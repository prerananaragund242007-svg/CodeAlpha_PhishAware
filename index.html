01 / KNOW THE THREAT

Understand the Threat.

Phishing is a social engineering attack in which criminals impersonate trusted sources to trick people into revealing information or taking unsafe actions.

✉

Email Phishing

Fake emails pretending to come from trusted organizations.

▣

Smishing

Fraudulent messages delivered through SMS or messaging apps.

☎

Vishing

Fraudulent phone calls designed to obtain private information.

◎

Spear Phishing

Targeted phishing messages designed for specific individuals.

LEARN FROM SCENARIOS

Phishing Case Studies

Explore common online scam scenarios and understand how to respond safely.

CASE 01

Fake Bank Alert

"Your bank account will be suspended today. Verify your details immediately."

Warning Signs

  • Creates fear and urgency.
  • Requests account verification.
  • Unexpected message.

Safe Response

Contact your bank using its official app or verified phone number.

CASE 02

Fake Delivery Message

"Your parcel is delayed. Pay a small fee to receive your delivery."

Warning Signs

  • Unexpected payment request.
  • Unknown delivery message.
  • Pressure to act quickly.

Safe Response

Check the delivery status through the official courier website.

CASE 03

Fake Internship Offer

"Congratulations! You are selected. Pay a registration fee to confirm."

Warning Signs

  • Unexpected selection message.
  • Payment demanded to secure a role.
  • No verified company details.

Safe Response

Verify the company and opportunity through its official channels.

CASE 04

Social Media Warning

"Your account violates our rules. Confirm your password to avoid suspension."

Warning Signs

  • Threat of account suspension.
  • Request for a password.
  • Unverified sender.

Safe Response

Open the social media app directly and check its official notifications.

TAKE ACTION

How to Report Phishing

Recognising a phishing attempt is important. Reporting it can help protect others too.

01. Do Not Interact

Avoid clicking suspicious links, downloading attachments or replying to unexpected messages.

02. Preserve Evidence

Keep relevant screenshots, messages, sender details and suspicious URLs. Do not share private information publicly.

03. Report the Incident

Use India's official cybercrime reporting portal to report cybercrime.

National Cyber Crime Portal ↗

04. Financial Cyber Fraud

If money has been lost through cyber fraud, contact your bank immediately and call the national helpline.

Always use official government websites and verified contact details.

LEARN BEFORE YOU CLICK

Phishing Knowledge Hub

Understand how online scams work and learn how to protect yourself in the digital world.

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of online scam where attackers pretend to be trusted people or organisations to trick users into sharing private information.

Common Types

  • Email phishing
  • SMS phishing (Smishing)
  • Voice phishing (Vishing)
  • Fake website phishing
  • Social media phishing

Social Engineering

Scammers use trust, fear, curiosity or urgency to influence people into making unsafe decisions.

Warning Signs

  • Unexpected messages
  • Urgent requests
  • Unknown senders
  • Suspicious links
  • Requests for passwords or OTPs

Real-World Examples

Fake delivery notifications, bank account warnings and fraudulent prize messages are common phishing scenarios.

Stay Protected

  • Verify the sender independently.
  • Check website addresses carefully.
  • Never share passwords or OTPs.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Report suspicious messages.
